
# Taking Lessons

Lessons are the individual learning units within each REGIMA training module. This guide will help you navigate and get the most from each lesson.

## Lesson Structure

Each REGIMA lesson is structured to provide comprehensive training on specific skincare techniques and includes:

### 1. Video Demonstration
- Featured video player with professional demonstration
- Title and description explaining the technique
- Playback controls for pausing and rewatching key moments

### 2. Technique Overview
- Detailed explanation of the skincare technique
- Scientific background and principles
- Context for when and why to use the technique

### 3. Key Benefits
- Bullet points highlighting the benefits of the technique
- Client results you can expect to achieve
- Differentiators from similar techniques

### 4. Step-by-Step Procedure
- Sequenced instructions for performing the technique correctly
- Numbered steps with clear actions
- Important notes and cautions for each step

### 5. Practical Demonstration
- Visual guides with images
- Before/after comparisons
- Technique application illustrations

### 6. Common Mistakes
- Guidelines on what to avoid
- Troubleshooting tips
- Client comfort considerations

## Interactive Elements

Lessons include interactive elements to reinforce your learning:

### Knowledge Check Quiz
- Multiple-choice questions to test your understanding
- Immediate feedback on your answers
- Score tracking and completion status
- Ability to retry if needed

## Resource Sidebar

Each lesson includes a sidebar with additional resources:

### Related Resources
- Downloadable PDFs for reference
- Additional video demonstrations
- Printable checklists and guides

### Featured Product
- Information about REGIMA products related to the lesson
- Product image and description
- Ingredient list
- Application instructions for professional use

## Lesson Navigation

To navigate through lessons:

1. Use the "Previous" and "Next" buttons to move between lessons
2. The "Return to Module" button takes you back to the module overview
3. Your progress is automatically saved as you complete each section

## Completing a Lesson

A lesson is considered complete when you:
1. View the video demonstration
2. Read through all content sections
3. Successfully pass the knowledge check quiz

When complete:
- A visual indicator will mark the lesson as finished
- The next lesson will be unlocked automatically
- Your progress will be updated in your overall module completion

## Practical Tips

- Take notes during video demonstrations
- Practice techniques immediately after learning them
- Use the knowledge checks to confirm your understanding
- Reference the sidebar resources for additional learning
- Return to completed lessons any time for review
