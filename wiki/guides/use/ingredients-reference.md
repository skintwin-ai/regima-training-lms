
# Using the REGIMA Ingredients Reference

The REGIMA Ingredients Reference is a comprehensive knowledge base designed to help you understand the science behind REGIMA's skincare formulations. This guide will show you how to effectively use this reference to enhance your understanding of skincare ingredients.

## Why Use the Ingredients Reference?

Understanding skincare ingredients helps you:

- Make informed product recommendations to clients
- Explain the science behind treatment protocols
- Address specific skin concerns with targeted ingredients
- Answer client questions about product formulations
- Enhance your professional knowledge and credibility

## Accessing the Ingredients Reference

### From the Training Portal

1. Log in to your REGIMA Training Portal account
2. Navigate to the main menu
3. Select "Ingredients" from the navigation sidebar
4. The ingredients reference will open in the main content area

### From Product Pages

When viewing product information, ingredient names are often linked directly to their reference entries. Click on any linked ingredient to view its detailed information.

### From Lesson Content

During lessons, when specific ingredients are mentioned, you'll often see a reference icon (🔍) next to the ingredient name. Click this icon to quickly access information about that ingredient.

## Using the Search and Filter Features

### Basic Search

The search function allows you to quickly find specific ingredients:

1. Enter an ingredient name (e.g., "Hyaluronic Acid") in the search box
2. The list will automatically filter to show matching ingredients
3. Click on any result to view detailed information

### Advanced Search Options

You can search by:
- Ingredient name
- Function (e.g., "exfoliation" or "brightening")
- Category (e.g., "antioxidant" or "peptide")
- Benefit (e.g., "hydration" or "anti-aging")

### Filtering by Category

Use the category filters to browse ingredients by type:

1. Click on a category in the sidebar (e.g., "Antioxidants")
2. The list will update to show only ingredients in that category
3. The number of ingredients in each category is displayed next to the category name

## Understanding Ingredient Information

Each ingredient entry contains several key sections:

### Overview
A brief description of the ingredient and its origin

### Classification
The ingredient's category, derivation, and other classification information

### Main Functions
The primary skincare benefits and mechanisms of action

### Skin Benefits
How the ingredient improves specific skin conditions or concerns

### REGIMA Formulation Notes
Special information about how REGIMA utilizes this ingredient, including proprietary technologies or delivery systems

### Professional Applications
How this ingredient is used in professional treatments

### Featured Products
Which REGIMA products contain this ingredient

## Making the Most of the Reference

### Study by Category

Take time to study ingredients by category to understand how different types of ingredients work:
- Antioxidants for environmental protection
- Peptides for collagen stimulation
- Exfoliants for cell turnover
- Hydrators for moisture retention

### Focus on Key Actives

Pay special attention to REGIMA's signature ingredients and proprietary complexes, as these are unique to the brand and important differentiators.

### Cross-Reference with Products

When studying a particular ingredient, note which products contain it to help build your product recommendation skills.

### Create Study Notes

Use the ingredients reference to create your own study notes or flashcards for ingredients you want to learn more about.

## Mobile Access

The ingredients reference is fully responsive and can be accessed on your mobile device:

1. Log in to the Training Portal on your smartphone or tablet
2. Navigate to the Ingredients section
3. The interface will adapt to your screen size for easy browsing

## Sharing Information

When discussing products with clients, you can use information from the ingredients reference to explain benefits. While you cannot share your login credentials, you can:

1. Take notes during your study sessions
2. Create simplified explanations for clients
3. Use the reference during consultations to access accurate information

## Regular Updates

The ingredients reference is regularly updated with:
- New ingredients as they are introduced to REGIMA formulations
- Additional research findings
- Enhanced information about existing ingredients

Check back regularly to stay informed about the latest ingredients knowledge.
