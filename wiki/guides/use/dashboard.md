
# Navigating the Dashboard

The dashboard is your starting point in the REGIMA Training Platform. It provides an overview of your progress and quick access to your training materials.

## Dashboard Overview

![Dashboard Screenshot]

The dashboard consists of several key components:

### 1. Welcome Header
At the top of the dashboard, you'll see a personalized welcome message with your name. This confirms you're logged in correctly to your account.

### 2. Course Progress Card
This card shows your overall progress through the REGIMA certification program:
- A visual progress bar indicating the percentage of modules you've completed
- Text showing "X of Y modules completed"
- "Continue Training" button to quickly resume where you left off

### 3. Achievements Card
This section displays your accomplishments:
- Number of certificates earned
- Access to view your certificates and achievements

### 4. Current Modules Section
Here you'll find modules you're currently working on:
- Module cards with titles and descriptions
- Progress indicators for each module
- Quick "Continue" buttons to resume your most recent modules

### 5. Module Tabs
You can switch between different views of your modules:
- **Current Modules**: What you're working on now
- **Upcoming Modules**: Modules that will be unlocked as you progress
- **Completed Modules**: History of modules you've already finished

## Mobile Navigation

On mobile devices, the dashboard adapts to a smaller screen:
- The sidebar is replaced with a menu toggle in the header
- Cards stack vertically for easier scrolling
- All functionality remains accessible through a mobile-optimized layout

## Quick Tips

- Click "Continue Training" to automatically go to your most recent lesson
- Check your progress regularly to stay on track with your certification timeline
- Access your achievements to view and download certificates you've earned
