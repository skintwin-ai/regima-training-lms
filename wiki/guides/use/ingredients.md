
# Using the Ingredients Reference

The Ingredients Reference is a comprehensive catalog of skincare ingredients used in REGIMA products. This guide will help you effectively use this feature to expand your skincare knowledge.

## Accessing the Ingredients Reference

The Ingredients Reference can be accessed from:
- The main navigation menu
- Related links within lesson content
- Product information pages

## Search and Filtering

### Using the Search Bar
The search function allows you to quickly find specific ingredients:
1. Enter an ingredient name, function, or category in the search bar
2. Results will appear as you type
3. Click on any result to view detailed information

### Filtering by Category
Use the category sidebar to browse ingredients by type:
1. Click on any category (e.g., "Exfoliants," "Antioxidants," "Proprietary Actives")
2. The ingredient list will filter to show only ingredients in that category
3. The number of ingredients in each category is displayed

### Active Filters
When filters are applied:
- Filter badges will appear showing your active filters
- Click the "Clear Filter" button to reset to all ingredients

## Understanding Ingredient Entries

Each ingredient is displayed in an expandable accordion format:

### Basic View (Collapsed)
- Ingredient name
- Primary category
- Brief description

### Detailed View (Expanded)
When you click on an ingredient, you'll see:
- **Concentration Range**: Typical percentages used in formulations
- **Main Functions**: Primary skincare benefits
- **Skin Benefits**: How it improves skin condition
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: Special information about how REGIMA utilizes this ingredient
- **Found In**: Products containing this ingredient

## Making the Most of the Reference

### Cross-Reference with Products
1. When viewing an ingredient, note which REGIMA products contain it
2. This helps you recommend the right products for specific skin concerns

### Education Support
Use the ingredients reference to:
- Prepare for client consultations
- Better understand lesson content
- Learn the science behind REGIMA formulations
- Answer client questions about product ingredients

### Integration with Training
The ingredients reference complements your training by:
- Providing deeper scientific background on techniques you learn
- Helping you understand why specific products are used in certain treatments
- Building your professional knowledge base

## Practical Tips

- Familiarize yourself with REGIMA's proprietary active ingredients
- Study ingredient categories most relevant to your specialization
- Use the reference during client consultations to explain product benefits
- Revisit the reference regularly to reinforce your ingredient knowledge
