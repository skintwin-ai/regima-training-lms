
# Using the Products Reference

The REGIMA Products Reference provides comprehensive information about skincare formulations and their appropriate applications. This tutorial will guide you through effectively using this feature.

## Accessing the Products Reference

1. Log in to your REGIMA Training Portal account
2. Click on "Products" in the main navigation menu
3. The Products Reference page will load showing all product categories

## Navigating the Interface

The Products Reference has three main sections:

1. **Search Bar** - Located at the top of the page
2. **Category Filter** - Located on the left side of the page
3. **Products List** - Occupies the main area of the page

## Using the Search Function

To find specific products:

1. Click the search bar at the top of the page
2. Type a product name, skin concern, or ingredient
3. The list will automatically filter as you type
4. Press Enter to complete your search

**Practice**: Search for "Derma Deep" and note how many results appear.

## Filtering by Category

To browse products by type:

1. Locate the category sidebar on the left side of the screen
2. Click on a category (e.g., "Cleansers" or "Serums")
3. The product list will update to show only products in that category
4. Notice the filter indicator showing your active filter
5. To clear filters, click the "X" on the filter indicator

**Practice**: Filter by "Serums" and note how many products are displayed.

## Exploring Product Details

To learn about a specific product:

1. Find a product in the list
2. Click on the product name or the expand arrow
3. The entry will expand to show detailed information:
   - Product overview
   - Key ingredients
   - Benefits
   - Usage guidelines
   - Professional notes
4. Click again to collapse the entry

**Practice**: Expand 3 different products and note their key ingredients.

## Understanding Product Cards

Each product card provides at-a-glance information:

1. **Product Image** - Visual reference of the packaging
2. **Product Name** - Official name of the REGIMA product
3. **Category Badge** - Indicates product type (e.g., Cleanser, Serum)
4. **Skin Type Icons** - Shows suitable skin types
5. **Key Concern Indicators** - Shows primary skin concerns addressed

## Connecting to Ingredients

To see detailed information about ingredients in a product:

1. Expand a product entry
2. In the Key Ingredients section, click on any linked ingredient name
3. You'll be taken to the detailed ingredient information page
4. Use the back button to return to the product

**Practice**: Find a product and click through to learn about one of its key ingredients.

## Using the Comparison Feature

To compare different products:

1. Select products for comparison by clicking the checkbox on each product card
2. Click the "Compare Selected" button that appears
3. View side-by-side comparison of selected products
4. Click "Clear Comparison" to start over

## Adding to Favorites

To bookmark important products for future reference:

1. Click the star icon on any product card to mark it as a favorite
2. Access your favorites by clicking "Favorites" in the sidebar
3. Remove from favorites by clicking the star icon again

## Viewing Usage Protocols

To see recommended application methods:

1. Expand a product entry
2. Scroll to the "Usage Guidelines" section
3. Review professional application and home care instructions
4. Note any special considerations or contraindications

## Finding Related Products

To discover complementary products:

1. Expand a product entry
2. Scroll to the "Complementary Products" section
3. View suggested products that work well together
4. Click on any related product to view its details

## Mobile Usage

The Products Reference works on mobile devices:

1. Access the portal from your mobile browser
2. Navigate to the Products section
3. Use the hamburger menu to access filters
4. Tap products to expand/collapse
5. Use portrait orientation for optimal viewing

## Study Tips

For effective learning:

1. Study products mentioned in your lessons
2. Focus on understanding key product functions and ingredients
3. Learn the professional application techniques
4. Understand which products work well together
5. Practice explaining product benefits in client-friendly language
