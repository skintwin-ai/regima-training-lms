
# Completing Lessons Tutorial

## Introduction

Lessons are the individual learning units within REGIMA training modules. This tutorial will help you navigate lessons effectively and get the most from each one.

## Accessing Lessons

1. Navigate to the Modules page
2. Click on a module that is unlocked
3. From the module detail view, click on an unlocked lesson
4. The lesson page will load with all content

## Understanding Lesson Structure

![Lesson Structure](https://example.com/lesson-structure.jpg)

Each REGIMA lesson is organized in a consistent structure:

### Video Demonstration Section
1. Located at the top of the lesson
2. Features a professional demonstration video
3. Includes title and description
4. Has standard video controls (play/pause, volume, fullscreen)

**Practice**: Play the video and use the pause button to stop at key moments.

### Content Sections
Below the video, you'll find several content sections:

1. **Technique Overview**:
   - Detailed explanation of the skincare technique
   - Scientific background and principles
   - Context for when and why to use the technique

2. **Key Benefits**:
   - Bullet points highlighting technique benefits
   - Expected client results
   - Comparisons with similar techniques

3. **Step-by-Step Procedure**:
   - Numbered instructions for performing the technique
   - Important notes and cautions
   - Timing guidelines for each step

4. **Practical Demonstration**:
   - Visual guides with images
   - Before/after comparisons
   - Technique application illustrations

5. **Common Mistakes**:
   - Guidelines on what to avoid
   - Troubleshooting tips
   - Client comfort considerations

**Practice**: Read through each section and make notes on key points.

## Using the Resource Sidebar

On the right side of the lesson, you'll find a resource sidebar:

1. **Related Resources**:
   - Download PDFs by clicking on document links
   - Access additional video demonstrations
   - Print checklists and guides for practical use

2. **Featured Product**:
   - Review the REGIMA product related to the lesson
   - See the product image and description
   - Study the ingredient list and application instructions

**Practice**: Download at least one resource to use as a reference.

## Completing Knowledge Checks

At the end of each lesson, you'll find a knowledge check quiz:

1. Answer each multiple-choice question
2. Submit your answers using the "Check Answers" button
3. Review your results:
   - Correct answers will be highlighted in green
   - Incorrect answers will show the correct option
4. If you don't pass (score below 70%), you can retry the quiz

**Practice**: Complete the knowledge check and review any incorrect answers.

## Navigating Between Lessons

At the bottom of each lesson, you'll find navigation buttons:

1. **Previous Lesson**: Takes you to the previous lesson in the module
2. **Next Lesson**: Becomes available after completing the current lesson
3. **Return to Module**: Takes you back to the module overview

## Completing a Lesson

A lesson is considered complete when you:
1. View the video demonstration
2. Read through all content sections
3. Successfully pass the knowledge check quiz (70% or higher)

When complete:
- A visual indicator will mark the lesson as finished
- The next lesson will be unlocked automatically
- Your progress will be updated in your overall module completion

## Practice Tips

For best results with your REGIMA training:
1. Take notes during video demonstrations
2. Practice techniques immediately after learning them
3. Use the knowledge checks to confirm your understanding
4. Reference the sidebar resources for additional learning
5. Return to completed lessons any time for review
