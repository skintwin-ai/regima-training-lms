
# Dashboard Navigation Tutorial

## Introduction

The dashboard is your central hub for managing your REGIMA skincare training. This tutorial will guide you through using the dashboard effectively to track your progress and access training content.

## Login and Access

1. Open your web browser and navigate to the REGIMA Training Platform
2. Enter your credentials (username and password)
3. Click "Sign In"
4. You will be automatically redirected to your dashboard

## Understanding Dashboard Sections

### Progress Overview

At the top of your dashboard, you'll find a visual summary of your training progress:

1. **Progress Cards**: Shows your overall completion percentage
2. **Next Lesson**: Displays your current or next lesson with a "Continue" button
3. **Recent Activity**: Highlights recently completed lessons and modules

![Dashboard Progress Section](https://example.com/dashboard-progress.jpg)

### Training Modules Section

In the main content area, you'll see available training modules:

1. Each module appears as a card with:
   - Module title
   - Brief description
   - Completion status (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed)
   - "Start" or "Continue" button

2. Modules are arranged in the required completion order
3. Completed modules show a green checkmark

## Navigating to Key Areas

### To Access Modules List:
1. Click on "Modules" in the sidebar menu
2. View all available modules in a comprehensive list

### To View Your Certificates:
1. Click on your profile picture in the top right
2. Select "My Certificates" from the dropdown menu
3. View and download certificates for completed modules

### To Update Your Profile:
1. Click on your profile picture
2. Select "Profile Settings"
3. Update your personal information and preferences

## Practice Exercise

Try these actions to become familiar with the dashboard:

1. Identify your current progress percentage
2. Locate your next lesson and click the "Continue" button
3. Find a completed module and review its content
4. Access your profile settings and review your information
