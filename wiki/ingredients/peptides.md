
# Peptides in Skincare

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that serve as building blocks for proteins like collagen, elastin, and keratin. In skincare, they act as messengers, signaling skin cells to perform specific functions such as collagen production, inflammation reduction, or muscle relaxation.
