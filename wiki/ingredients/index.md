
# REGIMA Skincare Ingredients

The REGIMA skincare line leverages powerful, scientifically-proven ingredients to deliver exceptional results. This reference guide provides detailed information about the key ingredients found in REGIMA products.

## Understanding Skincare Ingredients

REGIMA formulations are based on advanced skincare science, combining traditional botanical extracts with cutting-edge peptides, antioxidants, and other active compounds. Our ingredients are carefully selected and combined in optimal concentrations to maximize effectiveness while minimizing irritation.

## Ingredient Categories

REGIMA uses several categories of active ingredients:

- [Antioxidants](#antioxidants) - Help neutralize free radicals and protect skin
- [Peptides](#peptides) - Small proteins that signal skin cells and promote renewal
- [Exfoliants](#exfoliants) - Remove dead skin cells and promote cell turnover
- [Botanical Extracts](#botanical-extracts) - Plant-derived ingredients with multiple benefits
- [Vitamins](#vitamins) - Essential nutrients that support skin health
- [Hydrators](#hydrators) - Ingredients that attract and retain moisture
- [Proprietary Complexes](#proprietary-complexes) - REGIMA's exclusive ingredient combinations

## Key Ingredients

### Antioxidants

- **Vitamin C (L-Ascorbic Acid)** - Neutralizes free radicals, brightens skin tone, boosts collagen synthesis
- **Vitamin E (Tocopherol)** - Moisturizes skin while fighting free radicals and enhancing barrier function
- **Red Algae Extract** - Protects against environmental damage with powerful marine antioxidants

### Peptides

- **Biomimetic Peeling Peptide** - Enhances natural exfoliation without irritation
- **Palmitoyl Tripeptide-5** - Stimulates collagen production and improves skin elasticity
- **Biopeptide CL** - Promotes wound healing and skin repair

### Exfoliants

- **Glycolic Acid** - Alpha hydroxy acid that exfoliates the stratum corneum and improves skin texture
- **Lactic Acid** - Gentle alpha hydroxy acid that exfoliates while hydrating
- **Malic Acid** - Provides gentle exfoliation and enhances skin brightness

### Botanical Extracts

- **Centella Asiatica (Tiger Grass)** - Calms inflammation and supports wound healing
- **Arnica Montana Extract** - Reduces swelling and accelerates healing
- **Black Tea Extract** - Provides antioxidant protection and combats skin aging

### Vitamins

- **Niacinamide (Vitamin B3)** - Regulates sebum production, strengthens barrier function, and reduces redness
- **Vitamin A (Retinol)** - Accelerates cell turnover and stimulates collagen production
- **Vitamin C Derivatives** - Stable forms of vitamin C for brightening and protection

### Hydrators

- **Hyaluronic Acid** - Holds up to 1000x its weight in water for deep hydration
- **Snow Mushroom Extract** - Natural alternative to hyaluronic acid with smaller particle size
- **Glycerin** - Humectant that draws moisture to the skin

### Proprietary Complexes

- **Anti-Dark Circles Complex** - Targets under-eye darkness through multiple mechanisms
- **Collagen Renewal Complex** - Stimulates collagen production and preserves existing collagen
- **Detoxifier Complex** - Removes environmental pollutants and strengthens skin defenses
