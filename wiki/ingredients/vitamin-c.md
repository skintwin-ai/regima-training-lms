
# Vitamin C (L-Ascorbic Acid)

![Vitamin C](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/66f2cc_a34e4db9217b4b0a97884fe6db846a66%7Emv2.jpg)

## Overview

Vitamin C (L-Ascorbic Acid) is a potent antioxidant that plays a crucial role in skin health and is a cornerstone ingredient in many REGIMA formulations. This water-soluble vitamin is essential for collagen synthesis and provides powerful environmental protection.

## Classification
- **Category:** Antioxidant, Vitamin
- **Origin:** Naturally occurring vitamin, synthetically produced for skincare
- **pH Range:** Most effective at pH 3.5 or lower
- **Solubility:** Water-soluble

## Concentration in Formulations
REGIMA products utilize vitamin C at concentrations ranging from 5-20%, depending on the specific formulation and intended use. Professional-grade products may contain higher concentrations.

## Main Functions

- **Powerful Antioxidant Protection:** Neutralizes free radicals from UV exposure and environmental pollutants
- **Collagen Synthesis:** Stimulates fibroblasts to produce new collagen, essential for skin firmness
- **Tyrosinase Inhibition:** Blocks melanin production pathway to reduce hyperpigmentation
- **Anti-inflammatory:** Reduces skin inflammation and redness

## Skin Benefits

- **Brightening:** Effectively fades sunspots, melasma, and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation
- **Anti-aging:** Reduces fine lines and wrinkles by supporting collagen and elastin
- **Photoprotection:** Enhances sunscreen efficacy when applied underneath
- **Wound Healing:** Accelerates healing process and reduces scar formation
- **Texture Improvement:** Creates smoother, more refined skin texture

## REGIMA Formulation Approaches

REGIMA employs several strategies to maximize vitamin C stability and efficacy:

1. **Stabilized Formulations:** Combining with ferulic acid and vitamin E to enhance stability and effectiveness
2. **Vitamin C Derivatives:** Using stable forms like BV-OSC (tetrahexyldecyl ascorbate) for enhanced penetration
3. **pH Optimization:** Maintaining optimal pH levels for maximum activity
4. **Airless Packaging:** Preventing oxidation through special packaging design
5. **Synergistic Ingredients:** Pairing with complementary actives for enhanced results

## Professional Applications

Vitamin C features prominently in REGIMA professional treatments:
- **Brightening Protocols:** For pigmentation concerns
- **Anti-aging Facials:** To boost collagen production
- **Post-procedure Care:** After microneedling or peels to support healing and enhance results
- **Antioxidant Boosters:** To complement other professional treatments

## Contraindications and Considerations

- May cause mild tingling or redness, especially in higher concentrations
- Not recommended for use with niacinamide in the same application (can be used in separate steps)
- Store vitamin C products properly to prevent oxidation
- Patch test recommended for sensitive skin clients
