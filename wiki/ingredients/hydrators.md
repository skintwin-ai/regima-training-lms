
# Hydrating Ingredients in Skincare

Hydrators are ingredients that attract water to the skin and help maintain optimal moisture levels. They play a critical role in REGIMA formulations, supporting skin barrier function and enhancing the efficacy of other active ingredients.

## Types of Hydrating Ingredients

REGIMA formulations incorporate several categories of hydrating ingredients to address different aspects of skin hydration:

### Humectants

Ingredients that attract water molecules and bind them to the skin.

#### Hyaluronic Acid
- **Main Functions**: Attracts and binds water, plumps skin, creates moisture reservoir
- **Skin Benefits**: Intense hydration, reduces appearance of fine lines, soothes irritation
- **Concentration Range**: 1-2%
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: REGIMA uses multi-molecular weight hyaluronic acid complexes for penetration at different skin levels. Can hold up to 1000x its weight in water.

#### Glycerin
- **Main Functions**: Attracts water to skin surface, improves barrier function, enhances product spreadability
- **Skin Benefits**: Hydrates without oiliness, soothes sensitive skin, facilitates healing
- **Concentration Range**: 3-10%
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: Foundation humectant in many REGIMA formulations. Creates an ideal base for other active ingredients to perform optimally.

#### Snow Mushroom Extract
- **Main Functions**: Natural humectant, improves moisture retention, anti-inflammatory
- **Skin Benefits**: Deep hydration, reduces fine lines, soothes sensitive skin
- **Concentration Range**: 1-5% extract
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: Natural alternative to hyaluronic acid with smaller particle size for deeper penetration. Used in REGIMA's premium hydrating formulations.

### Emollients

Ingredients that soften and smooth the skin by filling in gaps between skin cells.

#### Marula Oil
- **Main Functions**: Rich in antioxidants, deeply moisturizing, enhances barrier repair
- **Skin Benefits**: Non-comedogenic hydration, protects against environmental damage, soothes inflammation
- **Concentration Range**: 3-100%
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: High in oleic acid (70-78%), making it particularly suitable for dry, mature skin types. REGIMA uses cold-pressed, unrefined oil for maximum nutrient retention.

#### Squalane
- **Main Functions**: Mimics skin's natural oils, seals in moisture, enhances barrier function
- **Skin Benefits**: Lightweight hydration, suitable for all skin types, improves elasticity
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: REGIMA sources plant-derived squalane from sustainable olive sources, providing the benefits of natural squalene without environmental concerns.

### Occlusives

Ingredients that create a protective seal over the skin to prevent water loss.

#### Shea Butter
- **Main Functions**: Locks in moisture, strengthens skin barrier, provides antioxidant protection
- **Skin Benefits**: Prevents moisture loss, soothes irritation, improves skin softness and elasticity
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: Used in specific concentration ranges to provide occlusion without heaviness in REGIMA's dry skin formulations.

#### Beeswax
- **Main Functions**: Creates protective barrier, prevents moisture loss, soothes skin
- **Skin Benefits**: Locks in hydration, protects against environmental aggressors, improves skin comfort
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: Used selectively in REGIMA lip treatments and intensive hand/foot care products.

## REGIMA's Multi-Dimensional Hydration Approach

REGIMA's hydration strategy works on multiple levels of the skin:

### Surface Hydration
- Immediate moisture replenishment
- Smoothing effect on skin texture
- Enhanced comfort and reduced tightness

### Dermal Hydration
- Deeper penetration of hydrating molecules
- Improved cellular function and communication
- Enhanced fibroblast activity for collagen production

### Barrier Enhancement
- Strengthening of the skin's natural moisture barrier
- Prevention of transepidermal water loss (TEWL)
- Increased resilience against environmental stressors

## Using Hydrators Effectively

For optimal results with REGIMA hydrating products:

1. Apply to slightly damp skin to maximize humectant effects
2. Layer products from lightest to heaviest consistency
3. Use humectants before emollients and occlusives
4. Increase hydration during dry weather or air travel
5. Combine with antioxidants for enhanced protection
