
# Exfoliants in Skincare

Exfoliants are ingredients that remove dead skin cells from the surface of the skin, promoting cell turnover and revealing fresher, newer skin cells. They are a critical component in many REGIMA professional treatments and home care products.

## Types of Exfoliants

REGIMA formulations utilize several categories of exfoliants, each with unique benefits and applications:

### Alpha Hydroxy Acids (AHAs)

Water-soluble acids derived from various sources that work primarily on the skin's surface.

#### Glycolic Acid
- **Main Functions**: Exfoliates stratum corneum, improves skin texture, accelerates cell turnover
- **Skin Benefits**: Reduces fine lines, brightens complexion, improves product penetration
- **Concentration Range**: 5-30%
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: Smallest AHA molecule for enhanced penetration. REGIMA professional treatments contain up to 30% concentration while retail products typically range from 5-10%.

#### Lactic Acid
- **Main Functions**: Gentle exfoliation, hydration, pH balancing
- **Skin Benefits**: Improves skin texture, enhances moisture retention, reduces sensitivity
- **Concentration Range**: 5-10%
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: Larger molecule than glycolic acid, providing gentler exfoliation. Also functions as a humectant. Ideal for sensitive skin types in REGIMA sensitive skin protocols.

#### Malic Acid
- **Main Functions**: Gentle exfoliation, skin brightening, antioxidant properties
- **Skin Benefits**: Provides gentle exfoliation, enhances skin brightness
- **Concentration Range**: 2-7%
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: Often used in combination with other AHAs for synergistic effects in REGIMA's multi-acid formulations.

### Beta Hydroxy Acids (BHAs)

Oil-soluble acids that can penetrate sebum and work within the pores.

#### Salicylic Acid
- **Main Functions**: Exfoliates within pores, reduces sebum production, anti-inflammatory
- **Skin Benefits**: Clears congestion, reduces acne lesions, refines pore appearance
- **Concentration Range**: 0.5-2%
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: Oil-soluble acid that penetrates sebum-filled follicles. REGIMA formulates with specialized delivery systems for enhanced penetration without irritation.

### Polyhydroxy Acids (PHAs)

A newer generation of exfoliants with larger molecular structures, providing gentler exfoliation.

#### Gluconolactone
- **Main Functions**: Gentle exfoliation, antioxidant properties, strengthens barrier function
- **Skin Benefits**: Suitable for sensitive skin, provides hydration, protects against oxidative damage
- **Concentration Range**: 3-8%
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: Used in REGIMA's sensitive skin formulations for gentle renewal without irritation.

### Enzyme Exfoliants

Natural enzymes that digest proteins, breaking down dead skin cells.

#### Pineapple Extract (Bromelain)
- **Main Functions**: Enzymatic exfoliation, smoothing, brightening
- **Skin Benefits**: Gentle non-abrasive exfoliation, improves tone and texture
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: REGIMA combines pineapple enzymes with papaya enzymes for comprehensive enzymatic exfoliation in professional treatments.

#### Papaya Extract (Papain)
- **Main Functions**: Enzymatic exfoliation, softening, smoothing
- **Skin Benefits**: Gentle non-abrasive exfoliation, unclogs pores
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: Works synergistically with bromelain in REGIMA's enzyme mask treatments.

## REGIMA's Biomimetic Exfoliation Approach

REGIMA has pioneered a biomimetic approach to exfoliation that mimics the skin's natural renewal processes:

### Biomimetic Peeling Peptide
- **Technology**: Peptides that enhance natural exfoliation by stimulating enzymatic activity within the skin
- **Benefits**: Provides exfoliation without direct acid application
- **Applications**: Ideal for extremely sensitive skin types that cannot tolerate traditional exfoliants

## Professional vs. Home Care Formulations

REGIMA differentiates between professional and home care exfoliant formulations:

### Professional Treatments
- Higher concentrations (up to 30% for glycolic acid)
- Precisely controlled pH levels
- Neutralization systems for safety
- Multiple acid combinations for enhanced results

### Home Care Products
- Lower concentrations (2-10% for AHAs)
- Buffered formulations for gradual release
- Often combined with soothing ingredients
- Designed for regular use within a complete regimen

## Using Exfoliants Safely

For optimal results with REGIMA exfoliant products:

1. Always perform a patch test before using a new exfoliant
2. Start with lower concentrations and gradually increase
3. Follow with appropriate moisturizers and sun protection
4. Use only as directed by a skincare professional
5. Discontinue use if irritation occurs
