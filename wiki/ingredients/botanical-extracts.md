
# Botanical Extracts in Skincare

Botanical extracts are plant-derived ingredients that provide a wide range of benefits for the skin. REGIMA harnesses the power of these natural compounds in many of its formulations, combining traditional plant wisdom with modern extraction technologies.

## Key Botanical Extracts in REGIMA Products

### Centella Asiatica (Tiger Grass)

- **Main Functions**: Anti-inflammatory, healing accelerator, antioxidant protection
- **Skin Benefits**: Calms redness and irritation, strengthens skin barrier, promotes collagen synthesis
- **Concentration Range**: 2-5% extract
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: REGIMA uses a proprietary complex of purified centella compounds (madecassoside, asiaticoside, madecassic acid, and asiatic acid) for enhanced efficacy.

### Arnica Montana Extract

- **Main Functions**: Anti-inflammatory, improves circulation, reduces swelling
- **Skin Benefits**: Reduces puffiness, accelerates healing, calms irritated skin
- **Concentration Range**: 1-3% extract
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: Key ingredient in REGIMA's lymphatic massage protocols. Used in specialized preparations for professional facial treatments.

### Black Tea Extract

- **Main Functions**: Antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, astringent
- **Skin Benefits**: Provides antioxidant protection, combats skin aging, tones skin
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: REGIMA uses fermented black tea extract (Kombuchka) for enhanced bioactivity and skin-renewing properties.

### Red Algae Extract

- **Main Functions**: Hydrating, protective, mineral-rich
- **Skin Benefits**: Protects against environmental damage with powerful marine antioxidants
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: Sustainably harvested from clean ocean waters and processed to preserve delicate bioactive compounds.

### Balloon Vine Extract

- **Main Functions**: Anti-inflammatory, soothing, barrier strengthening
- **Skin Benefits**: Reduces irritation, calms sensitive skin, enhances moisture retention
- **REGIMA Formulation Notes**: Used in REGIMA's sensitive skin formulations for its exceptional calming properties.

## Extraction Methods

REGIMA employs several extraction methods to maximize the efficacy of botanical ingredients:

### Cold-Pressed Extraction
- Preserves heat-sensitive compounds
- Maintains the natural integrity of the plant material
- Used primarily for carrier oils in REGIMA formulations

### CO2 Extraction
- Uses carbon dioxide under pressure to extract compounds
- Produces highly pure extracts without solvent residues
- REGIMA uses this method for delicate aromatic compounds

### Water Extraction
- Uses water as the extraction medium
- Captures water-soluble plant compounds
- Used for many herbal extracts in REGIMA products

### Glycolic Extraction
- Uses glycerin as the extraction medium
- Preserves both water and oil-soluble compounds
- Provides added humectant benefits in the final formulation

## Sustainability Practices

REGIMA is committed to sustainable sourcing of botanical ingredients:

1. **Traceability**: All botanical ingredients can be traced back to their source
2. **Ethical Harvesting**: Plants are harvested at optimal times using sustainable methods
3. **Organic Preference**: When possible, REGIMA sources organically grown botanicals
4. **Fair Trade**: Support for fair compensation to farmers and suppliers
5. **Biodiversity**: Protection of native habitats and plant diversity

## Clinical Validation

REGIMA's botanical extracts undergo rigorous testing:

- In vitro testing to confirm bioactivity
- Ex vivo testing on skin explants
- In vivo testing with volunteer panels
- Stability testing under various conditions
