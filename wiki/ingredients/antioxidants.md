
# Antioxidants in REGIMA Formulations

Antioxidants are a cornerstone of REGIMA skincare formulations, providing essential protection against environmental damage and free radicals.

## What Are Antioxidants?

Antioxidants are compounds that inhibit oxidation, a chemical reaction that can produce free radicals and chain reactions that may damage cells. In skincare, antioxidants help protect the skin from environmental aggressors like UV radiation, pollution, and other sources of oxidative stress.

## Key Antioxidants in REGIMA Products

### Vitamin C (L-Ascorbic Acid)

**Main Functions:**
- Neutralizes free radicals
- Brightens skin tone
- Boosts collagen synthesis

**Benefits:**
- Reduces hyperpigmentation
- Protects against UV damage
- Improves skin firmness

**Concentration:** 5-20%

**REGIMA Formulation Notes:** REGIMA uses stabilized forms of vitamin C combined with ferulic acid and vitamin E for enhanced efficacy and stability. This powerful combination provides superior antioxidant protection.

### Vitamin E (Tocopherol)

**Main Functions:**
- Neutralizes free radicals
- Moisturizes skin
- Enhances barrier function

**Benefits:**
- Soothes inflammation
- Improves skin hydration
- Heals skin barrier

**Concentration:** 0.5-1%

**REGIMA Formulation Notes:** Often paired with vitamin C in REGIMA products to enhance stability and effectiveness of both ingredients. This oil-soluble antioxidant works synergistically with other antioxidants.

### Red Algae Extract

**Main Functions:**
- Provides powerful antioxidant protection
- Supports cellular renewal
- Enhances skin resilience

**Benefits:**
- Protects against environmental damage
- Improves skin texture
- Supports natural regeneration processes

**Concentration:** 1-5%

**REGIMA Formulation Notes:** REGIMA sources high-quality red algae extract for its exceptional antioxidant properties and ability to thrive in extreme environmental conditions, transferring this resilience to the skin.

### Kombuchka (Black Tea Ferment)

**Main Functions:**
- Provides antioxidant protection
- Improves skin firmness
- Enhances radiance

**Benefits:**
- Brightens the complexion
- Smooths fine lines
- Refines skin texture

**Concentration:** 2-5%

**REGIMA Formulation Notes:** This fermented black tea extract contains potent antioxidants that help combat glycation, a key factor in skin aging.

## How Antioxidants Work in REGIMA Products

REGIMA's formulation philosophy includes strategic layering of different antioxidants to provide comprehensive protection. Many REGIMA products contain multiple antioxidant ingredients that work synergistically to:

1. Neutralize various types of free radicals
2. Provide both immediate and long-lasting protection
3. Support skin's natural defense mechanisms
4. Enhance the efficacy of other active ingredients

## Professional Application

For skincare professionals, understanding antioxidants is crucial for:
- Creating effective treatment protocols
- Recommending appropriate home care
- Addressing photodamage and environmental aging
- Complementing other treatments such as peels or microdermabrasion

REGIMA's professional treatments often incorporate antioxidant-rich products as both treatment and post-procedure protection.
