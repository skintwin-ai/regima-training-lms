
# Lesson Feature

The lesson page provides structured learning content for individual REGIMA skincare training lessons.

## Content Sections

- **Video Demonstration**: Featured video player with title and description
- **Technique Overview**: Detailed explanation of skincare techniques
- **Key Benefits**: Bullet points highlighting benefits of the technique
- **Step-by-Step Procedure**: Sequenced instructions for performing the technique
- **Practical Demonstration**: Visual guides with images showing proper technique
- **Common Mistakes**: Guidelines on what to avoid during application

## Interactive Elements

- **Knowledge Check Quiz**: Multiple-choice questions to test understanding
  - Question display with options
  - Immediate feedback on answers
  - Score tracking and completion status

## Resource Sidebar

- **Related Resources**: Downloadable PDFs, videos, and checklists
- **Featured Product**: Information about REGIMA products related to the lesson
  - Product image
  - Ingredient list
  - Application instructions

## Navigation

- Previous/Next lesson buttons
- Return to modules navigation
- Conditional navigation based on lesson sequence

## Progress Tracking

- Automatic tracking of lesson completion
- Integration with overall module progress
- Visual indicators of completion status
