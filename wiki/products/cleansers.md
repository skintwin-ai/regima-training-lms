
# REGIMA Cleansers

REGIMA offers a range of specialized cleansers designed to address different skin types and concerns while preparing the skin for treatment products.

## Cleanser Types

### Derma Deep Rich Creamy Cleanser

![Derma Deep Rich Creamy Cleanser](/siteref/render/Home%20-%20RegimARegimA_files/Product-Home-Page-Product-Page-Derma-Deep-Rich-Creamy-Cleanser-22-November-2017-1170-x-500px-870x500.jpg)

**Overview**: A luxurious cream cleanser that gently removes impurities without stripping the skin's natural protective barrier.

**Key Ingredients**:
- [Hydrolyzed Wheat Protein](/wiki/ingredients/hydrators.md): Forms a protective film while moisturizing
- [Glycerin](/wiki/ingredients/hydrators.md): Hydrates and softens the skin
- [Arnica Montana Extract](/wiki/ingredients/botanical-extracts.md): Soothes inflammation and redness

**Benefits**:
- Thoroughly cleanses without drying
- Maintains skin pH balance
- Prepares skin for treatment products
- Suitable for sensitive and dry skin types

**Professional Notes**: 
Ideal pre-treatment cleanser for clients with dry, sensitive, or mature skin. Can be used in combination with gentle exfoliants for enhanced penetration of active ingredients.

### Derma Zest Purifying Gel Cleanser

**Overview**: A refreshing gel cleanser that deeply cleanses while controlling excess oil and refining pores.

**Key Ingredients**:
- [Bitter Orange Extract](/wiki/ingredients/botanical-extracts.md): Natural astringent properties
- [Tea Tree Oil](/wiki/ingredients/botanical-extracts.md): Antibacterial and purifying
- [Salicylic Acid](/wiki/ingredients/exfoliants.md): Gently exfoliates and unclogs pores

**Benefits**:
- Deep cleansing action for oily and combination skin
- Helps prevent breakouts
- Refines the appearance of pores
- Leaves skin feeling fresh without tightness

**Professional Notes**:
Recommended pre-treatment cleanser for acne-prone and oily skin types. Use before professional chemical peels to ensure thorough removal of surface oils.

### Advanced Enzymatic Cleanser

**Overview**: An innovative dual-action cleanser that combines gentle surfactants with natural enzymes to cleanse and exfoliate simultaneously.

**Key Ingredients**:
- [Papain (Papaya Enzyme)](/wiki/ingredients/exfoliants.md): Natural enzymatic exfoliant
- [Bromelain (Pineapple Enzyme)](/wiki/ingredients/exfoliants.md): Breaks down dead skin cells
- [Centella Asiatica Extract](/wiki/ingredients/botanical-extracts.md): Calms and supports healing

**Benefits**:
- Gentle enzymatic exfoliation
- Brightens dull complexion
- Enhances penetration of treatment products
- Suitable for most skin types except very sensitive

**Professional Notes**:
Excellent pre-treatment cleanser that performs mild exfoliation. Not recommended before chemical peels as it may increase sensitivity.

## Cleanser Selection Guide

### By Skin Type

| Skin Type | Recommended Cleanser |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Dry | Derma Deep Rich Creamy Cleanser |
| Oily | Derma Zest Purifying Gel Cleanser |
| Combination | Derma Zest Purifying Gel Cleanser (T-zone) with Derma Deep for dry areas |
| Normal | Any cleanser based on preference |
| Sensitive | Derma Deep Rich Creamy Cleanser |
| Mature | Derma Deep Rich Creamy Cleanser |

### Pre-Treatment Recommendations

| Treatment Type | Recommended Cleanser |
|----------------|----------------------|
| Chemical Peels | Derma Deep or Derma Zest (avoid Advanced Enzymatic) |
| Microdermabrasion | Any cleanser appropriate for skin type |
| LED Therapy | Any cleanser appropriate for skin type |
| Microneedling | Derma Deep Rich Creamy Cleanser |

## Usage Guidelines

### Professional Application
1. Apply to damp skin with light circular motions
2. Allow to remain on skin for 1-2 minutes for optimal effect
3. Remove with warm water and sponges or soft cloths
4. Pat skin dry before proceeding with treatments

### Home Care Instructions
Instruct clients to:
1. Use morning and evening
2. Massage gently for 30-60 seconds
3. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water
4. Follow with appropriate REGIMA toner and treatment products

## Clinical Insights

REGIMA cleansers are formulated with an optimal pH range (4.5-5.5) to maintain the skin's acid mantle while effectively removing impurities. The formulations avoid harsh surfactants like sodium lauryl sulfate that can compromise barrier function.

## Cross-Selling Opportunities

Each cleanser is designed to work synergistically with corresponding REGIMA treatment products:
- Derma Deep pairs well with moisturizing serums and creams
- Derma Zest complements oil-control and acne-targeted treatments
- Advanced Enzymatic Cleanser enhances brightening and anti-aging regimens
