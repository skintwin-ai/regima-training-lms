
# REGIMA Serums

REGIMA serums are highly concentrated formulations designed to target specific skin concerns with powerful active ingredients. These professional-grade products deliver noticeable results through advanced delivery systems.

## Core Serum Line

### 24-Hour Chronoactive Serum

**Overview**: A comprehensive serum that addresses multiple signs of aging with a time-released formula that works continuously throughout day and night cycles.

**Key Ingredients**:
- [Vitamin C (L-Ascorbic Acid)](/wiki/ingredients/vitamin-c.md): Brightens and stimulates collagen
- [Palmitoyl Tripeptide-5](/wiki/ingredients/peptides.md): Promotes collagen synthesis
- [Hyaluronic Acid](/wiki/ingredients/hydrators.md): Provides deep hydration
- [Red Algae Extract](/wiki/ingredients/antioxidants.md): Protects against free radical damage

**Benefits**:
- Reduces appearance of fine lines and wrinkles
- Improves skin firmness and elasticity
- Enhances radiance and evens skin tone
- Provides 24-hour antioxidant protection

**Professional Notes**: 
A versatile serum suitable for most clients over 25. Can be incorporated into treatment protocols and recommended for daily home care.

## Application Techniques

### Layering Guide

REGIMA serums are designed to be layered with other products. The general rule is to apply products from thinnest to thickest consistency:

1. Cleanse and tone
2. Apply water-based serums
3. Apply oil-based serums
4. Apply moisturizer
5. Apply sunscreen (daytime only)

### Professional Application

For in-clinic treatments:
1. Apply 3-5 drops to cleansed skin
2. Use gentle pressing motions rather than rubbing
3. Allow 1-2 minutes for absorption before applying additional products
4. For enhanced penetration, consider using with microcurrent or ultrasound devices

### Home Care Instructions

Instruct clients to:
1. Apply to clean, slightly damp skin
2. Use 2-3 drops for entire face
3. Press gently into skin rather than rubbing
4. Allow to absorb before applying moisturizer
5. Use morning and/or evening as directed
